# OctoAcme Project Management Documentation

## Executive Summary
OctoAcme uses a structured, customer-first project management approach designed for iterative delivery, clear ownership, and measurable outcomes. Teams work through a consistent lifecycle from project initiation to retrospective, with shared artifacts, defined roles, and predictable communication rhythms. This docs hub is the central entry point for understanding how projects are planned, executed, released, and continuously improved.

## Core Principles
- **Customer-first**: Prioritize customer value and usability.
- **Iterative delivery**: Deliver small, testable increments.
- **Clear ownership**: Ensure each project has named delivery and product owners.
- **Data-informed decisions**: Measure impact and adjust based on evidence.
- **Psychological safety**: Encourage transparent feedback, learning, and blameless improvement.

## Project Lifecycle Overview
OctoAcme project delivery follows five phases:

1. **Initiation**: Define the problem, stakeholders, scope boundaries, and initial success criteria.
2. **Planning**: Break work into increments, prioritize backlog, map dependencies, and align milestones.
3. **Execution**: Deliver iteratively, track progress, manage blockers, and maintain quality.
4. **Release**: Validate readiness, complete release checks, deploy, and communicate outcomes.
5. **Close & Retrospective**: Capture learnings, assign improvement actions, and feed insights into future work.

## Communication Cadence
- **Weekly PM + PdM syncs** for delivery/risk alignment
- **Twice-weekly or regular standups** for team execution tracking
- **Monthly stakeholder updates** for progress, decisions, and roadmap confidence
- **Ad-hoc escalations** for urgent risks, incidents, or dependency blockers

## Process Summary
OctoAcme uses a structured lifecycle approach with clear phase gates and lightweight artifacts to keep delivery predictable while remaining adaptive. Work moves from validated initiation to scoped planning, then into iterative execution, release readiness, and close-out with retrospective learning.

Role clarity is central to the model: PMs coordinate delivery, scheduling, and risk management; PdMs own product direction, prioritization, and outcome measurement; Developers design and implement solutions with testing and review discipline; and QA validates quality and acceptance criteria before release.

Communication is intentional and rhythmic, combining weekly alignment, team standups, and monthly stakeholder reporting with templates for status and incident communication. Quality assurance is built into execution through acceptance criteria, code review, and testing practices. Risks are tracked through a risk register with explicit owners, mitigations, and defined escalation paths.
